Rules

  1. Four technicians walk to real source/turnout fixtures to operate them. Ordinary rail cells remain walkable on a separate technician layer.
  2. A launch spends one of eight charges of that colour. Existing pulses advance one edge per step; a new pulse starts at the source until the next step.
  3. A departing pulse reads the OLD turnout mode. A same-step SET applies only to subsequent departures.
  4. Only the current receiver demand is visible nearby. Correct colours advance its three-step prefix; wrong or unnecessary arrivals consume the pulse without progress.
  5. Role maps and entity locations must use their actual radius-two visibility masks. Full-state/debug views are privileged; future recipes are never scene attributes.
  6. Event outcomes, pulse identities and rail direction annotations are debug diagnostics, not extra policy columns. The policy receives only the declared 557 columns.
  7. Blue and amber groups are disjoint masks over the same sixteen physical slots. Follow unique pulse IDs when a consumed slot is reused.
  8. Arrival events carry actual from/to nodes. No radio, collision, recall, fabricated delivery or hypothetical successful-run animation is permitted.
  9. All receivers complete means success; empty stock with no pulses and incomplete receivers means failure. Natural outcomes precede the 512-step limit.
  10. Terminal states are absorbing. Consume a recorded event only once per t; full-state diagnostics must not masquerade as a role observation.

## 旧/新状态与并行动作

行走和设施操作都读取动作开始时的位置。不能在同一步先走近再操作。同一分流器只收到单一 SET 值时接受，收到两种值时互相冲突并保持旧状态。多个源旁玩家同色请求只产生一枚脉冲，不按玩家 ID 分配奖励；异色请求本步取消发射。

先按旧分流器状态推进全部旧脉冲，再按旧当前需求处理接收、清除已消耗槽。之后才能分配一个空槽给新发射；新脉冲留在源节点，不能提前移动。最后提交新分流器设置、工人位置、库存和冷却。

这种顺序意味着“脉冲离开分流器的同一步才设置”不能改变该脉冲，却能改变后来脉冲。进入分流器节点时还未决定下一条边。冷却在发射后设为 2，其他步骤递减到 0，所以最短发射间隔是三步。

在合法可达状态中，单源、无合流轨道与同速传播确保一个接收站同一步至多收到一枚脉冲。代码利用该设计不变量并行汇总，而不按存储槽顺序人为引入竞争。该不变量仍须后续运行检查，本文未执行检查。

`accepted_arrivals/12 + 1.0*natural_success - 0.001 - 0.02*wasted_arrivals`

错误颜色和投递已完成站都计为浪费。每站最多三个正确前缀步骤，库存不可再生，因此不存在重复切换分流器的进度刷分。
