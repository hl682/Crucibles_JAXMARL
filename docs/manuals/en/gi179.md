## Objective and gameplay

Deliver three distinct loads to their designated stations while preserving quality; original issuers earn private priority-weighted quality and the actual carrier earns a handling bonus.

Finite dusty loads and carried burden dirty shared cells; filters can clean a peer route, expose a peer to quality loss, or be saved for private use. Real load ownership can be handed to a waiting adjacent courier.

- Three couriers share three narrow crossings in a public partition. Pollution is sensed within Manhattan radius two; outside the mask it is unknown.

- Movement costs one stamina, or two when the target old dust is at least two. Old occupied cells block movement; lower agent id wins a common empty destination.

- Moving finite dusty loads and carried burden deposits dust at departure. Deposits cap at three; overflow is counted as vented, then filters sweep, then final-position contact occurs.

- A filter neutralizes one contacted unit. Unfiltered contact damages carried quality; available body capacity takes one dust unit. Waiting recovers stamina but still contacts dust.

- Deliveries credit the original private-priority issuer, the actual carrier and a small shared receipt bonus. A waiting empty neighbor may accept a handoff.

- AUTHOR SOURCE ONLY: no local visibility replay, animation, causality run, successful delivery or learning result has been observed.

Ending: All three loads delivered, otherwise H=96 truncation. Zero quality is retained and may still be delivered.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Complementary production & service: A useful outcome requires ordered stages, scarce equipment or another role's capability. Measure real completed products and resource circulation rather than counting intermediate actions as success.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi179")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 251; actions are scalar int32; masks have 11 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
