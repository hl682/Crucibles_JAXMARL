## Objective and gameplay

Individually bank three harvested tufts while preserving a viable shared meadow; decide when to extract, restore, protect, donate and move away from crowded patches.

Two herders may harvest the same adjacent patch in one tick; their combined withdrawals and local crowding suppress future growth. Restoring a patch, protecting it during stewardship, or giving inventory to a neighbor changes that neighbor future resources and feasible actions.

- A herder can hold three tufts and bank three at the shared depot.

- Harvest targets the nearest unprotected living patch within one cell; equal distances use patch id.

- Sowing spends one bag tuft to restore one standing tuft. The current steward sets a shorter recovery timer.

- The steward rotates every eight ticks and may mark an adjacent patch fallow for four ticks.

- Regrowth needs two retained tufts, zero recovery timer, and at most one herder within one cell.

- Six consecutive bare ticks permanently kill a patch; sowing before loss can preserve its roots.

- Harvests, gifts, seeds and bank deposits conserve harvested inventory; ecological growth is separately counted.

- Rewards: -0.001 per tick, +0.05 harvest, -0.03 sow, +1 bank, -0.5 collapse or +0.25 sustainable completion.

- Terminal conditions are source game rules; they are not scientific acceptance thresholds.

Ending: Collapse terminates when every patch has dead roots. Sustainable completion terminates when each agent has banked three, every patch retains living roots and total standing biomass is at least twelve. Otherwise truncate at 144.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Survival, protection & rescue: Protection and repair alter another role's future feasible actions. Record losses, exposure, rescue and infrastructure as well as delivery; survival is determined by the actual kernel.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi151")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 138; actions are scalar int32; masks have 12 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
