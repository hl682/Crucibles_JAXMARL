# GI106 · Hose Relay: Firebreak Rescue

Rescue six evacuees before accumulating three severe-fire exposures, using shared water, hose infrastructure and firebreaks.

## Objective and gameplay

Rescue six evacuees before accumulating three severe-fire exposures, using shared water, hose infrastructure and firebreaks.

Stationary laid hoses propagate pressure only while a nearby responder cranks the finite pump. Leaving a relay changes downstream water access; cutting nonburning brush changes subsequent fire spread. Consensual handoffs change who can evacuate and maintain infrastructure.

- Move on the 9 by 7 world; intensity-2 or intensity-3 fire blocks entry. Multiple responders may share a tile.

- Lay hose while stationary. The tender reaches four Manhattan cells; other endpoints reach three. Pump pressure propagates through connected laid hoses.

- A nearby responder must crank the pump each supply tick. The mechanic doubles per-recipient flow; the finite reservoir is consumed in rotating recipient order.

- Spraying consumes one stored water and reduces the hottest adjacent fire; cutting removes only nonburning fuel. The tender cuts a cross of five cells.

- Every fourth transition adjacent fire may ignite fueled cells. Burning consumes fuel; three severe-fire exposures lose an uncarried evacuee.

- Pick up at an evacuee tile and deliver at the shelter. Both adjacent responders must choose handoff to exchange a carried evacuee.

- The rescue carrier moves every tick while carrying; the other two move on even input ticks. Rescue and loss affect all rewards.

Ending: All six evacuees are either rescued or lost. Mixed rescues and losses are valid terminal outcomes. Otherwise truncate at 96 transitions; natural completion has priority.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Survival, protection & rescue: Protection and repair alter another role's future feasible actions. Record losses, exposure, rescue and infrastructure as well as delivery; survival is determined by the actual kernel.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi106")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 242; actions are scalar int32; masks have 11 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
