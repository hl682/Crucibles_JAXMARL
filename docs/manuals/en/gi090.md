## Objective and gameplay

Two teams of two compete to carry the ball into their own far goal after a teammate has caught a pass during the current possession.

Real one-cell ball flight can be intercepted; adjacent opposing tackles transfer possession. Passing changes the receiver's scoring opportunity. Body occupancy, stamina, brace timing and a four-step hold limit change partner and opponent routes.

- Roles 0 and 1 form team 0; roles 2 and 3 form team 1. A goal requires a completed teammate pass during the current possession.

- Movement costs one stamina; wait and brace restore one up to four. Old occupied cells and conflicting destinations block movement.

- A cardinal pass flies one cell per later step, up to four cells. Any runner on its current cell catches it; opponents erase relay eligibility.

- An adjacent tackle spends two stamina. A bracing holder with positive previous stamina resists; possession held for four further owner steps spills.

- The first qualified goal ends play. Natural completion takes priority at the horizon; the raw terminal state is absorbing.

Ending: The first relay-qualified goal ends the episode. The scoring team's two roles receive +1 each and the other team -1 each; all other rewards are zero. Otherwise the episode truncates at step 128.

## What this game explores

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Team opposition & interception: Cooperation within a team can coexist with opposition between teams. Preserve team membership, capture and possession rules, and report each team's outcomes instead of assuming a single shared return.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi090")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 126; actions are scalar int32; masks have 8 entries. Supply all 4 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
