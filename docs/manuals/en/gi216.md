## Objective and gameplay

Maintain and trade between four settlements while choosing resource investments, levee/culvert routing, raised roads and shared flood responses during a persistent 20x20 interaction window.

Levees and closed culverts divert physical packets toward neighboring fields. Roads allow deep-water crossings and faster hauling but degrade in severe water. Grain trade and material gifts change other settlements' future construction/food options; dated reports require physical inspection or adjacent transfer.

- Four settlements own separate stone, timber and grain stores. A worker carries at most four resource units.

- A levee costs two stone; a raised road costs two timber. Convert a levee into a controllable culvert using one of each.

- Water packets move south every second tick. A closed barrier sends them outward sideways, or holds them if that exit is blocked.

- Deep water blocks ordinary movement. Raised roads allow crossing and two-cell hauling, but depth three damages them on periodic pulses.

- Field wetness is the maximum capped depth within two cells of a settlement. Deep exposure loses grain and crops.

- Depositing foreign grain pays trade value and benefits the recipient; harvesting another field removes its actual grain.

- Local reports preserve their observation dates when handed to a neighboring worker. Remote current water is not revealed by old reports.

- Public aggregate flood alerts and distinct food valuations coexist with private stores and local views. The world truncates at 384.

Ending: No single settlement quest ends this persistent strategy world. terminated remains false and tick 384 truncates; harvest, trade, construction and flood losses never reset the state.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Information, sensing & communication: Who knows a fact, when it was measured, and whether it was communicated are separate state variables. Test memory or communication using only the same role-visible evidence.

Complementary production & service: A useful outcome requires ordered stages, scarce equipment or another role's capability. Measure real completed products and resource circulation rather than counting intermediate actions as success.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Survival, protection & rescue: Protection and repair alter another role's future feasible actions. Record losses, exposure, rescue and infrastructure as well as delivery; survival is determined by the actual kernel.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi216")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 3001; actions are scalar int32; masks have 24 entries. Supply all 4 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
