## Objective and gameplay

Fulfil three distinct private workshop orders using conserved raw stock, shared heat, cooling, a press and one transport/handling tool.

Any worker can spend or vent common heat, reserve shared machines/tongs, consume coolant, deliver or supply components, and change another worker future recipe timing. Transfers and two-piece assembly depend on partner positioning and waiting. Personal completion plus shared bonuses creates mixed incentives.

- Each worker privately requires one annealed, tempered or composite product; reset permutes one of each order among workers.

- Six raw material units are conserved across active, delivered and consumed slots. Composite assembly moves two units into one slot.

- Each worker carries at most one workpiece. Shared tongs are a separate single-owner resource.

- Stoking consumes one of six fuel and raises kiln heat by three, capped at eight. Venting lowers it by two.

- The kiln loses one heat every fourth tick. Coolant replenishes one every eighth tick, capped at four.

- Raw loading requires heat at least three; at heat above four it requires tongs. Processing takes two ticks with tongs or three manually.

- Outside the kiln, hot material loses one heat every third tick, or every sixth tick when carried by the tongs holder; zero heat becomes annealed.

- Quenching costs one coolant. Peak heat at least six and current heat at least three make tempered output; otherwise output is annealed.

- Press two annealed one-unit pieces, one carried and one nearby on the floor; processing takes two ticks with tongs or three manually.

- Tongs used by kiln or press remain occupied until completion. Completed output and tongs eject at the station for anyone to collect.

- Only the correct private product is accepted at dispatch. Completion gives the worker one and all workers 0.2; fuel use costs its user 0.02.

- Workers arbitrate resources in rotating clock order. All three orders complete ends naturally; horizon 144.

Ending: All three order-completed flags true. Otherwise H=144 truncates; shortage is not automatically declared impossible.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Information, sensing & communication: Who knows a fact, when it was measured, and whether it was communicated are separate state variables. Test memory or communication using only the same role-visible evidence.

Commitment, exchange & governance: Promises consume time, liquidity, access or material. Separate an announced agreement from a binding commitment and from its eventual delivery; log cancellation and expiry too.

Complementary production & service: A useful outcome requires ordered stages, scarce equipment or another role's capability. Measure real completed products and resource circulation rather than counting intermediate actions as success.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi125")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 351; actions are scalar int32; masks have 11 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
