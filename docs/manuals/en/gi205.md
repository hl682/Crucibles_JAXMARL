## Objective and gameplay

Bring six produce units to the community barn while tending shared pollination routes, moisture and finite planting resources.

Opening or repairing a physical hedge gate changes worker and bee routes. Carrying a shelter changes its bees' home and suspends foraging. Early harvesting removes nectar immediately, changing other bees' target choices and delaying or enabling neighboring crop development. Water, strips and reseeding require real labor and finite supplies.

- Workers walk actual soil and open gates. Each can carry one shelter or one produce batch, plus up to two water units. All roles can tend neighboring patches.

- Open a healthy gate from within one cell. Open gates wear every fourth tick; one wood repairs to three. Closing never traps a worker or bee occupying the gate cell.

- Each shelter has two bees. Carrying it makes those bees return toward its real moving position; deploying it changes their foraging neighborhood.

- Bees walk one legal cell per tick. They choose reachable nectar within distance six of their shelter, using bounded path distances and a six-tick foraging budget before returning.

- Two visits and three watered growth ticks ripen a bloom. Harvesting bloom early gives one produce; ripe harvest gives two. Either removes that patch's nectar.

- Water crops or strips from carried supply; wells replenish every fourth tick. Bloom ages out at eighteen, drought stress three kills, and strips expire at twelve.

- Plant a flowering strip at clear current soil or reseed a spent patch with one finite seed. Strips can attract bees away from crops as well as change their travel.

- Store produce privately at your stall or donate at the barn. Local visibility and native action masks apply equally to human and policy roles.

Ending: Success when community produce totals reach six. Failure when all crop patches are harvested or dead, no seeds remain and no produce is carried, unless success. Otherwise truncate at 168.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Information, sensing & communication: Who knows a fact, when it was measured, and whether it was communicated are separate state variables. Test memory or communication using only the same role-visible evidence.

Complementary production & service: A useful outcome requires ordered stages, scarce equipment or another role's capability. Measure real completed products and resource circulation rather than counting intermediate actions as success.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Survival, protection & rescue: Protection and repair alter another role's future feasible actions. Record losses, exposure, rescue and infrastructure as well as delivery; survival is determined by the actual kernel.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi205")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 317; actions are scalar int32; masks have 16 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
