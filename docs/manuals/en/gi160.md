Your two-player team incapacitates both opponents while using shared shields, ammunition and rescues to remain active.

## Objective and gameplay

Your two-player team incapacitates both opponents while using shared shields, ammunition and rescues to remain active.

At least two adjacent identical push commands move a shield before fire; the move can expose a teammate. Bodies and shields block rays. Teammates can transfer ammunition or revive prior casualties.

- Teams are guards zero and one versus guards two and three. Each starts with two health, six energy and two rounds.

- Rail shields move one cell when at least two adjacent guards agree on direction and that direction has a unique largest vote. Operators stay in place.

- Every push attempt spends one energy. Shields cannot enter an occupied cell, wall or another shield; simultaneous shield destination conflicts cancel both moves.

- Walking happens after shield motion, then supply transfers, then all shots resolve from the resulting positions. Walking cannot enter a starting occupied cell.

- A shot spends a round and one energy, sets a two-tick cooldown and travels at most six cells. The first wall, shield or body stops it. Friendly bodies block without damage.

- Rest recovers two energy up to eight. Each team has six reserve rounds at its base. Adjacent teammates can donate ammunition.

- An active guard can spend one round and two energy to revive an adjacent teammate that was down before the transition. A newly hit guard cannot rescue if it is now down.

- Both enemy guards down wins; both teams eliminated in the same transition draws. Terminal raw state is preserved. Horizon: one hundred twenty-eight transitions.

Ending: Either team has both health values zero after simultaneous fire and eligible rescues. Simultaneous elimination is a draw. Otherwise horizon truncation.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Team opposition & interception: Cooperation within a team can coexist with opposition between teams. Preserve team membership, capture and possession rules, and report each team's outcomes instead of assuming a single shared return.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi160")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 218; actions are scalar int32; masks have 16 entries. Supply all 4 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
