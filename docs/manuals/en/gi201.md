## Objective and gameplay

Deliver six total fruit portions through individual owner stalls or the community barn while growing saplings, managing shade, water and finite planting beds.

A screen's actual footprint protects a young neighbor from hot-phase stress while denying light to mature trees in that column. Rotating, carrying or dropping screens changes peer growth and heat exposure. One shared water cart, spring regeneration and a single carrying slot compete with planting, harvest and private/community delivery.

- A gardener carries one sapling, tool or fruit portion. Shared work cannot bypass that physical carrying slot.

- A deployed screen shades the column one cell to its east/west, up to two rows above or below. Carried screens cast no shade.

- Rotate or relocate a screen to protect young plants in hot phase, accepting reduced light and growth for shaded neighbors.

- Young and mature plants each require four lit, watered growth ticks to advance. Night and shade stop growth.

- Every third tick plants use one water; hot unshaded plants use one extra. Drought or unshaded young heat increases stress.

- Stress three kills the plant. Healthy conditions reduce stress by one; dead plants keep occupying their finite bed.

- The single cart carries four water. Springs hold six and regenerate one every third tick.

- Owner stalls give an owner bonus; the community barn shares delivery reward. Either route counts toward the six-portion task.

Ending: Success when private plus community deliveries total at least six. Failure only when every plant identity is dead and no gardener carries fruit, with success taking priority if already achieved.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi201")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 443; actions are scalar int32; masks have 13 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
