## Objective and gameplay

Two teams capture the enemy physical beacon and carry it onto their own base while their own beacon is on its home tile and uncarried.

Bodies block movement; axial tags stun opponents and drop their cargo; same-team pass plus receive transfers a beacon; signals share live radar but reveal the transmitter; decoys jam hostile radar near them and absorb a closest tag beam.

- Teams (0,1) and (2,3) capture and return the enemy beacon while their own beacon is home.

- Scouts have radius-four radar; couriers radius two. Walls constrain movement, not radar.

- Transmit shares the sender radar with its teammate for three ticks and publicly reveals the sender.

- Each role can deploy one four-tick physical decoy. It hides nearby bodies/cargo from distant hostile radar and absorbs a nearest tag beam.

- Tags travel along a clear row or column at most two tiles, stun for two future ticks, and drop carried cargo. A fired tag has three-tick cooldown.

- Interact within one tile to recover your grounded beacon or pick up the enemy beacon.

- Offer plus an adjacent teammate receive action transfers a carried beacon; a unilateral offer does not.

- Same destination and previously occupied-cell movement attempts are blocked. Tags resolve simultaneously; object contests use role id order.

- The first capture ends play: +1 to each winner, -1 to each opponent. Otherwise step 96 truncates.

- Map, home coordinates, scores and event identities are public. Positions and attributes obey the actual role visibility masks.

Ending: The first transition with any valid capture ends the joint episode; otherwise horizon 96 truncates. A natural capture on step 96 takes priority.

## What this game explores

Information, sensing & communication: Who knows a fact, when it was measured, and whether it was communicated are separate state variables. Test memory or communication using only the same role-visible evidence.

Commitment, exchange & governance: Promises consume time, liquidity, access or material. Separate an announced agreement from a binding commitment and from its eventual delivery; log cancellation and expiry too.

Complementary production & service: A useful outcome requires ordered stages, scarce equipment or another role's capability. Measure real completed products and resource circulation rather than counting intermediate actions as success.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Team opposition & interception: Cooperation within a team can coexist with opposition between teams. Preserve team membership, capture and possession rules, and report each team's outcomes instead of assuming a single shared return.

Survival, protection & rescue: Protection and repair alter another role's future feasible actions. Record losses, exposure, rescue and infrastructure as well as delivery; survival is determined by the actual kernel.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi102")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 181; actions are scalar int32; masks have 11 entries. Supply all 4 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
