Use shared hedges, blankets, hoe, watering can and moving pollinator to grow six original plants, harvest them and carry their produce to the village.

## Objective and gameplay

Use shared hedges, blankets, hoe, watering can and moving pollinator to grow six original plants, harvest them and carry their produce to the village.

Moving a hedge alters wind shelter, moving sunlight shadows, walking space and the insect route. A blanket preserves a bed against frost while preventing growth, pollination, tending and harvesting until physically removed. Shared single tools and one-item hands require labor and inventory handoffs.

- Six original crops share two movable hedges, two blankets, one hoe and one watering can. Each gardener can carry one object.

- Place a hedge on an adjacent non-bed tile, or a blanket directly onto an adjacent growing bed. Pick a blanket up to uncover it.

- A hedge one column upwind protects nearby beds against frost, but casts a moving one-to-two-cell horizontal shadow.

- Blankets prevent frost, growth, insect pollination, tending and harvest while covering a bed.

- The pollinator moves along a physical perimeter route and reverses when a standing hedge blocks its next cell.

- Sunny uncovered crops consume tilled soil to grow. Stages beyond three require pollination; daylight also dries soil moisture.

- Days have seven light ticks and five cold ticks. Wind reverses each day; six cumulative unprotected frost ticks kill a plant.

- Use the shared hoe and can, harvest mature pollinated crops, and carry produce to the village crate. Exhausted crops plus no loose produce ends the episode.

Ending: Natural end when every bed is harvested or dead and no active produce remains in the world. This includes completed delivery and crop-loss exhaustion; otherwise truncate at tick 180.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi213")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 265; actions are scalar int32; masks have 16 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
