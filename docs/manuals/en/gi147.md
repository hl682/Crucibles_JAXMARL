# GI147 · Harbor Barter and Complementary Cargo

Fulfil the boat's private two-type delivery demand by consensual cargo exchange and use of two congested shared docks.

## Identity and original contract

3 independently actionable roles; 14 discrete commands per role; 146 native observation columns per role; original horizon 90 steps. Declared per-step reward range: -1 to 8. Observation regime: `partially_observable`.

Source SHA-256: `644e8a7b3ff57a0ef176b7eb23e05748452733331f04a1f279793e500c796109`

The objective, rewards, population, map and horizon remain original. A preview is not a success claim.

## Objective and gameplay

Fulfil the boat's private two-type delivery demand by consensual cargo exchange and use of two congested shared docks.

Each boat starts with two same-type items and capacity two. Mutual named acceptance exchanges exactly one advertised item each. A withheld offer or refusal can leave a partner without a necessary type. Shared dock leases and physical blocking delay competing deliveries.

- Six individual cargo items exist, two of each type. Every boat starts with two items and can carry at most two.

- Each boat privately requires two different types. Missing types are permuted at reset, preserving two demands per type.

- Advertise a held type for four ticks. A trade needs live offers and both adjacent boats explicitly accepting each other.

- Trades swap one item each and conserve item identities. Refusal, expiration or withdrawal prevents the trade.

- Entering a shallow approach forces one recovery tick before the next move.

- Old dock leases govern movement. New three-tick reservations apply after movement; simultaneous requests use cyclic priority.

- Moves into occupied old cells or contested destinations fail. Completed boats depart and have only wait.

- Delivery consumes a demanded item into a permanent dock ledger. Individual reward is six per delivery, three on completion and minus one per active turn.

Ending: All three boats complete their private demands, otherwise horizon ninety. A completed boat leaves the collision world and has only NOOP until joint completion. Natural completion takes priority at the horizon.

## What this game explores

Information, sensing & communication: Who knows a fact, when it was measured, and whether it was communicated are separate state variables. Test memory or communication using only the same role-visible evidence.

Commitment, exchange & governance: Promises consume time, liquidity, access or material. Separate an announced agreement from a binding commitment and from its eventual delivery; log cancellation and expiry too.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi147")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 146; actions are scalar int32; masks have 14 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
