## Objective and gameplay

Two teams contest one ball. A team wins by carrying a relayed ball into its opposite-end strip; direct solo carries cannot score.

Flight interception, adjacent tackle/brace contests, shared destination conflicts and receiver positioning alter ownership, relay eligibility and scoring opportunities.

- Teams are players 0/1 versus 2/3. Carry to x=10 or x=0 at y=2..4 after a different teammate receives a pass.

- Moves cost one stamina; passes and tackles cost two; brace costs one; rest restores two, capped at six.

- A pass advances one grid cell per transition for three cells. Standing on it catches it; bracing reaches one adjacent cell.

- Moves into currently occupied cells are illegal. Shared empty-target moves are all rejected; swaps and following are blocked.

- Movement resolves before tackles, then pass flight/catches. Brace wards tackles. Catch and tackle ties use rotating initiative.

- The first qualified goal terminates; otherwise 64 steps truncate. Source and scripts are authored, not executed.

Ending: The first relay-qualified goal ends the match, including at t=64. Otherwise t=64 truncates.

## What this game explores

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Team opposition & interception: Cooperation within a team can coexist with opposition between teams. Preserve team membership, capture and possession rules, and report each team's outcomes instead of assuming a single shared return.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi138")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 64; actions are scalar int32; masks have 11 entries. Supply all 4 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
