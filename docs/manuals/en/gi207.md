## Objective and gameplay

Serve two vegetable and two dense baskets using finite ingredients, wrappers, fuel and wash water.

Opening one physical tier permits peer handling while venting pressure used by the other. The rolling pin, finite wrappers, single counter and boiler water create shared preparation/cooking choices; overfilling can spoil another cook's basket.

- One basket or rolling pin fits in each worker's hands. Nearest accessible pickup uses distance then object ID; lids must be open for tier pickup.

- Roll raw baskets on the counter while holding the pin. Vegetable needs one roll, dense two; completed preparation consumes one clean wrapper.

- Two physical tiers share pressure. Any open lid subtracts two pressure per tick; a fueled wet burner adds one up to three.

- Closed tiers cook only with pressure at least two and boiler water one through four. Vegetable accepts ticks four to six, dense seven to nine.

- The burner consumes finite fuel. Every third heating tick evaporates one water; three dry-heating danger ends the shift.

- Water above four wets loaded baskets, and overflow wets them further. Moisture three spoils a prepared or ready batch.

- Serve at the hatch; the basket becomes dirty. Wash at the sink using one flask unit, or discard a bad batch there.

- Actions and state are identical for human and policy roles; scripts are unexecuted source hypotheses.

Ending: Success is two accepted orders of each filling. Dry-heating danger three or insufficient remaining unspoiled portions for either order is failure unless success. Otherwise cap at 180.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi207")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 172; actions are scalar int32; masks have 17 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
