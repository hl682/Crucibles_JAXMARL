## Objective and gameplay

Repair both facilities to four units by transporting and installing limited cables, switches and batteries. Every crew member receives total actual repair increment divided by eight.

Installed cables and switches define a seven-node graph. Enabled batteries supply their actual connected components. Demand exceeding three units per available battery trips the component. Node 6 opens the gate; nodes 4 and 5 power repair tools. Specialty-limited installation and physical handoff make partners change one another inventory, routes and work opportunities.

- Connect upper, lower or central routes using six cable tokens and two switch tokens.

- The rigger installs cables, the specialist installs switches, and the courier installs batteries. Any role may carry and hand over a part.

- Two batteries each hold at most twelve charge and can be installed at source node 0 or central node 6.

- Closed connected paths distribute power. Each available battery supports three demand units; excessive load trips connected batteries for three ticks.

- A loaded enabled battery spends one charge, or up to two on overload. A courier can pedal one charge into a carried battery near source 0.

- A working facility requests two power units; the central gate requests one. Idle and completed facilities request none.

- Adjacent powered service supplies two repair units from the specialist or one from another role.

- The middle passage opens only while node 6 has power; upper and lower walking routes remain alternatives.

- Finish four units at both facilities. Reward records actual repair increments only.

Ending: Both facilities reach four repair units; otherwise time ends at 112 transitions. These task rules are not scientific performance gates.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi119")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 355; actions are scalar int32; masks have 12 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
