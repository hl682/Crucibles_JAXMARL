# GI115 · Freight canal locks and shared water

Deliver each boat cargo to its indexed dock using either lock lane and the two shared transferable water units.

## Identity and original contract

3 independently actionable roles; 11 discrete commands per role; 100 native observation columns per role; original horizon 128 steps. Declared per-step reward range: -1.0 to 10.0. Observation regime: `fully_observable`.

Source SHA-256: `dd21f93f71a3b24167e86e3dc1098b05bc286ee88e0cb5c489b32f83427dd360`

The objective, rewards, population, map and horizon remain original. A preview is not a success claim.

## Objective and gameplay

Deliver each boat cargo to its indexed dock using either lock lane and the two shared transferable water units.

A chamber may be opened at the low bank only at level zero and at the high bank only at level two. Filling or transferring water changes what the other chamber can do. Boats physically occupy cells, may form convoys and may need to yield an entrance to allow opposing traffic.

- Left basin is low and right basin is high. Each lock chamber has a low gate, a high gate and a level from zero to two.

- Only two water units exist across both chambers and the shared reservoir. Filling, draining and inter-chamber transfer conserve their sum.

- A boat may command a lane while at x=3 through 7 on its lock row, including the chamber and gate approaches.

- One command wins per lane by rotating boat priority; lane processing order alternates to settle shared-reservoir conflicts.

- Closed gates permit pumping. A low gate opens only at level zero; a high gate opens only at level two.

- Boats occupy cells. The rotating move order permits following an already vacated cell but blocks occupied destinations and direct swaps.

- Each own delivery gives six reward units and each team delivery gives every boat one; waiting and pump attempts incur costs.

- No executed convoy, numerical conservation trace or learning result is claimed by this author package.

Ending: All three boats delivered; otherwise truncate at tick 128, with natural completion taking priority on that tick.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

For every owned role choose a legal command and press `Queue`. Arrow keys / WASD queue a declared directional action for the current role camera when available. Space or `Advance one joint step` advances everyone together. Non-directional games use the selectors.

Unqueued roles use the labelled untrained uniform-legal policy. Take over or release roles before the next step without resetting. Full-state debug is privileged and permanently logged; role cameras preserve permissions. Close the session to retain logs. Server restart does not restore sessions.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi115")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 100; actions are scalar int32; masks have 11 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
