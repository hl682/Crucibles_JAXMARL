## Objective and gameplay

Harvest the assigned crop while deciding how much finite upstream water and personal effort to spend on shared canal service and neighbours.

A shutter physically redirects travelling packets before they can reach later gardens; upstream obstructions destroy downstream supply. Any gardener can maintain mouths or pour stored water into another garden.

- Water is released one packet per step from a finite reservoir and travels along the canal.

- An open sluice diverts that packet to its garden; upstream use delays or removes downstream supply.

- Condition zero destroys passing packets. Nearby maintenance restores condition at an individual cost.

- Gardens store six water units; each fourth tick wet living crops grow, and twelve consecutive dry ticks cause permanent wilt.

- Carry up to four water units, pour into a neighbour garden, or harvest your ripe crop from an adjacent cell.

- All resolved gardens end the game; the 64-step limit and terminal absorption preserve the actual final state.

Ending: All three gardens have been harvested or permanently wilted; otherwise the natural state is truncated at 64 raw steps.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Complementary production & service: A useful outcome requires ordered stages, scarce equipment or another role's capability. Measure real completed products and resource circulation rather than counting intermediate actions as success.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi162")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 118; actions are scalar int32; masks have 10 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
