## Objective and gameplay

Prepare vegetables and protein, combine one of each on each of three trays, heat full trays, and carry them to the serving counter before spoilage.

Every rotation moves every attached item, changing other cooks' reachable ingredients and a full tray's exposure to the single north heater. Shared assembly and floor handoffs compete with carousel timing.

- Three cooks share six ingredients, three trays and one eight-slot serving carousel.

- West board prepares vegetables; east board prepares protein. Add one prepared component of each type to a nearby unattended tray.

- Any cook beside the ring can request rotation. Opposite requests cancel; net speed is capped at one slot per tick.

- Putting or taking an item happens before rotation. Every attached item then moves with its logical slot.

- A full tray at the north heat slot gains one heat per tick. A held full tray can also be heated manually beside that station.

- Serve full trays at heat 3..6. Heat above six or filled-tray age 45 permanently spoils that tray.

- Carrying items through the outer corridor avoids ring displacement but uses travel and inventory time.

- Three served trays or exhaustion of unspoiled trays ends naturally; tick 180 otherwise truncates.

Ending: Natural completion at three served orders, or natural exhaustion when no active unspoiled tray remains. Otherwise truncate at tick 180.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi211")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 232; actions are scalar int32; masks have 15 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
