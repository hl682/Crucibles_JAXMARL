# GI190 · River Charter

Manage a finite eight-flood river economy. Earn personal utility by eating grain or exporting it, while diversion, bridge access and shipments change other polities resources and opportunities.

## Objective and gameplay

Manage a finite eight-flood river economy. Earn personal utility by eating grain or exporting it, while diversion, bridge access and shipments change other polities resources and opportunities.

Envoys walk the real map. Foreign bridge entry transfers a grain toll; sluice settings allocate a shared four-unit upstream flow; physical barges deliver downstream; town unloads transfer grain without gift reward.

- Lead one of three river settlements. Use walk actions to move your cloaked envoy; all three players choose one native action per step.

- Stand on your town to load grain or eat it. Carry at most 2. Unload at any town to transfer grain, or sell at the eastern market.

- At your dock, build a bridge for 2 grain. An entering foreign envoy pays its owner 1 stored grain while its toll pennant is raised.

- At your dock, toggle diversion. Every eighth step four water units flow north to south; each diverting settlement takes up to 2 and grows that much grain.

- An open-sluice pledge costs 1 grain and locks diversion off for the next 2 floods. The remaining-flood counter is real state.

- Launch cargo at your dock: barges advance one river cell per step. Upper and middle shipments supply the next town; the lower barge exports at y=8.

- Granaries, satchels, sluice handles, bridge pennants and loaded boats show actual state. Ownership follows north-to-south role order.

- A role sees its own consumption taste. Human controls must use that role observation and native legal mask; fixed scripts are examples only.

- After the eighth flood, the game ends when outstanding boats arrive, or at the horizon. No successful replay or learned strategy is claimed.

Ending: Natural completion is eight completed floods and no barge still in transit. Otherwise H=88 truncates, with natural priority and absorbing terminal state.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Commitment, exchange & governance: Promises consume time, liquidity, access or material. Separate an announced agreement from a binding commitment and from its eventual delivery; log cancellation and expiry too.

Complementary production & service: A useful outcome requires ordered stages, scarce equipment or another role's capability. Measure real completed products and resource circulation rather than counting intermediate actions as success.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

For every owned role choose a legal command and press `Queue`. Arrow keys / WASD queue a declared directional action for the current role camera when available. Space or `Advance one joint step` advances everyone together. Non-directional games use the selectors.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi190")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 49; actions are scalar int32; masks have 14 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
