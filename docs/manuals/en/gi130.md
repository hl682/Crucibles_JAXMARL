## Objective and gameplay

Deliver the three finite cargo boats to basin 3 while irrigating each indexed crop twice or recording its loss. Each role benefits especially from its same-index cargo and crop; sharing and bridge access affect all roles.

A gate simultaneously controls a walking bridge, one conserved delayed water packet, and boat passage. Irrigation and emergency drainage remove water needed for later downstream cargo. Close votes veto opens. Shared crop and spill allocations rotate by t modulo three.

- Move on banks and closed gate bridges. Opening a gate prevents bridge entry and permits downstream water and cargo.

- Every open edge can dispatch one existing donor unit; the packet arrives on the next live transition. New arrivals cannot depart in the same transition.

- Close votes win simultaneous open/close conflicts. Crop and spill contention use cyclic role priority.

- Launch nearby boats. A launched boat crosses one open lock per turn only when both endpoint depths lie from one through four.

- Irrigation permanently consumes a water unit. Two doses finish a crop. An unfinished crop loses one health at basin depth five or six.

- Intentional spilling prevents flooding but permanently removes scarce water. Delivered cargo, irrigated crops and crop losses affect owner and shared rewards.

Ending: All three boats delivered and every crop either has two doses or zero health. Otherwise the horizon is 160 live transitions. A dead crop remains an explicit negative outcome.

Action timing: Every indexed intent is legal for a live capable role, even when its spatial/resource precondition fails; failed intents become deterministic no-ops. Out-of-range actions become wait. Terminal roles, and temporarily/incapacitated roles where specified, retain wait only. Masks do not leak private feasibility.

## What this game explores

Resource commons & externalities: An action changes a persistent resource or burden that another role also encounters. Compare extraction with maintenance and record the downstream resource trajectory.

Commitment, exchange & governance: Promises consume time, liquidity, access or material. Separate an announced agreement from a binding commitment and from its eventual delivery; log cancellation and expiry too.

Complementary production & service: A useful outcome requires ordered stages, scarce equipment or another role's capability. Measure real completed products and resource circulation rather than counting intermediate actions as success.

Joint transport & shared access: Positions, load geometry, occupancy and persistent access constrain whose action can succeed next. Distinguish physical handoff, shared controls and simultaneous force from a cosmetic cooperation label.

Team opposition & interception: Cooperation within a team can coexist with opposition between teams. Preserve team membership, capture and possession rules, and report each team's outcomes instead of assuming a single shared return.

Survival, protection & rescue: Protection and repair alter another role's future feasible actions. Record losses, exposure, rescue and infrastructure as well as delivery; survival is determined by the actual kernel.

These are questions supported by rule structure, not observed learning effects.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi130")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 158; actions are scalar int32; masks have 10 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
