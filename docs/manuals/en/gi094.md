## Objective and gameplay

Cooperate to complete the public sequence of four flavor-specific orders using physical raw ingredients, two cleaver work units, four oven ticks and delivery at dispatch.

A single cleaver, one shared oven tile, consented adjacent handoffs and a one-cell central doorway couple task division. Occupancy can block partners; cumulative heat and aging continue while other roles act.

- Move ingredients through cutting, oven and dispatch stages in the public flavor order.

- The shared cleaver is required for two cutting actions at the bench. Cooks may carry a cleaver and one ingredient.

- Drop a cut ingredient on the oven. Four cumulative oven ticks bake it; eight burn it. Picked ingredients also spoil after 50 active ticks.

- Adjacent cooks must both choose handoff; an ingredient may be handed off at most once per step.

- Movements into old occupied tiles or competing destinations fail. The central doorway therefore requires scheduling.

- Each delivery gives every cook +1; each spoiled ingredient gives every cook -0.5. All resolved ingredients end the game.

- Role views show the public state. These rules and scripts are authored hypotheses, not measured outcomes.

Ending: All four ingredients are resolved, whether delivered or spoiled. Horizon 96 otherwise; natural completion has priority at the horizon.

Action timing: Every integer command in [0,U) is legal for every role before joint termination. Geometrically blocked or unavailable operations are deterministic failed attempts. Out-of-range commands become action 0. In absorbing states only action 0 is legal; raw_step ignores all supplied actions and preserves terminal state.

## Reinforcement-learning integration

```python
import jax
from crucibles_jaxmarl import make

env = make("gi094")
key = jax.random.PRNGKey(0)
key, rk, ak, sk = jax.random.split(key, 4)
obs, state = jax.jit(env.reset)(rk)
actions = env.sample_actions(ak, state)
obs, state, rewards, dones, info = jax.jit(env.step_env)(sk, state, actions)
done = dones["__all__"]
```

Observations are float32 vectors of width 143; actions are scalar int32; masks have 10 entries. Supply all 3 roles each transition. Masked sampling is an engineering reference, not a trained learner.

`step_env` retains terminal data without reset. `step` performs outer autoreset and keeps terminal data in `info["raw_state"]` and `info["raw_observation"]`. Use `dones["__all__"]` for episode boundaries; separate completion, failure and time limit in analysis.

JIT or vmap equal identities and shapes. Split independent reset, policy and transition keys. Group different shapes explicitly; do not shrink the population or horizon. Host rendering stays outside training.
