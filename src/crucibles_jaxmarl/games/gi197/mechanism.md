# Meadow Rotations

AUTHORED_NOT_EXECUTED; train-only attempt_00. No runtime, compatibility, replay, solvability, learning or transfer claim.

Native dimensions A=3, O=222, U=12, H=128. Humans and policies use the same reset/raw_step/observe/legal_actions. Human UI and public adapter are later integration work.

Meadow Rotations is a warm rural pasture game with three named-owner herds, six grass patches, two troughs, four gates, two carrying tools and a well/barn pair. Fixed vertical fences at x=3 and x=6 divide the board. Gate states change the actual map tiles used by shepherd and herd movement. Northern and southern gate alternatives allow different walking routes instead of a single abstract social switch. Herds may share a cell with a shepherd, enabling physical collection; herds and shepherds each have unique positions within their own group.

A shepherd has one carrying slot: either one milk portion or one tool. The two tools are the single bucket and single repair hammer, and their positions follow real holders. Taking/dropping uses the shepherd's exact cell. At Manhattan distance at most one from a gate, action 5 toggles the lowest-index eligible gate with positive condition. At most one toggle per gate is processed in a tick; competing requests can fizzle. Herd entry onto a gate consumes one condition. Zero condition forces it open. A charged hammer repairs the lowest-index adjacent gate below six, restoring up to three condition for one charge. Repair does not automatically close a gate. The barn holds three finite spare boards, each reloading one hammer charge, while the initial hammer contains two charges.

The bucket holds at most three servings and refills only at the explicit unlimited well. Pouring transfers as many servings as fit into the lowest-index adjacent nonfull trough, capped at six. Water is neither generated by an idle trough nor automatically returned after drinking. Carrying the bucket means forgoing milk collection until it is handed off by a physical drop. This permits a traveling water tender and stationary collectors, or workers rotating those jobs.

After shepherd actions and map refresh, the clock advances. Hunger increases on every even resulting t and thirst on every third, each capped at eight. Herds move on even ticks in rotating order (new_t + k) mod 3. A herd with thirst at least four chooses a trough maximizing three times current water minus Manhattan distance; otherwise it chooses a plot maximizing twice current grass minus distance. It then chooses among stay/east/north/west/south using smallest distance to that target, rejecting blocked tiles and another herd's current cell. Ties use that listed order. This is a visible local greedy rule, not a pathfinder; gate choices can create detours or a blocked direct route.

A herd on a grass cell with nonzero hunger consumes one portion and removes up to three hunger. A herd on a trough with nonzero thirst consumes one serving and removes up to four thirst. These spatial consumptions can occur on nonmovement ticks too. A plot with any herd within Manhattan distance one resets rest to zero; otherwise rest increases to a maximum two. Rest at two permits one grass regrowth each tick, capped at six. On every fourth tick, a need of eight costs one health; a living herd with hunger and thirst at most three produces one milk if stored milk is below three. Health never silently regenerates.

Milk collection removes one actual herd portion and keeps its original owner in the carrier state. Delivering at the barn credits that owner, whoever performed the labor. An eastern shepherd opening the central-eastern gate can change the central owner's herd route and future pasture/water demand. Leaving that gate shut protects resting pasture but sends the herd elsewhere; repairing it preserves future closure control. These choices interact with water-tender time, scarce tool possession, grazing pressure and delayed produce. No success, adaptation or mechanism-effect measurement is claimed.

Reward is -0.01 per active role per step, plus 0.2 to every role for each delivered portion, plus 1 to that portion's original herd owner, plus 2 to all on joint success or minus 2 to all on health failure. There is no reward for repeated toggling, refilling, repair or tool transfer.

## Actions

- 0: Wait
- 1: North
- 2: East
- 3: South
- 4: West
- 5: Toggle an adjacent gate with positive condition
- 6: Repair adjacent gate with charged held hammer
- 7: Collect one nearby herd milk portion with empty hands
- 8: Deliver held milk at barn (4,6)
- 9: Take tool on own cell or put held tool on an unoccupied tool cell
- 10: Fill held bucket at well (0,3), or load held hammer boards at barn
- 11: Pour held bucket into adjacent nonfull trough

## Information and boundary

Fully observable. Every role receives its one-hot and all public obs_keys leaves: actual map, shepherds and their cargo, herd coordinates/owners/needs/health/milk, gate condition/open state, grass and rest, trough water, tool locations/holders/charges, facilities, boards and delivery/production events. Herd, tool and scene information is the same for humans and policies. No hidden animal target or private planner is exposed through rendering; target selection is recomputed directly from visible native state.

Masked/invalid actions become wait. Old occupied agent cells cannot be entered, even if vacated simultaneously; shared destinations use rotating priority (role - old_t) mod 3. Roles remain active until joint termination and terminal masks permit only wait. Leaves are int32/bool and observations/rewards float32. Signed IDs are int32 losslessly and keys are not copied into state. Reset and all transitions are deterministic. The required key arguments are accepted but unused.

Failure when any herd health reaches zero. Otherwise success when every owner's delivered count is at least two. Failure excludes simultaneous success; either natural cause takes priority over the cap. Nonterminal calls advance once. Natural causes outrank the cap. Terminal inputs preserve state/clock/observation/causes and return zero reward/all dones true without resetting.

## Invariants and limits

All agents and herds remain in board bounds; each group has unique positions. Gate wear stays 0..6; zero wear implies open. Grass stays 0..6, rest 0..2, trough water 0..6, each tool charge 0..3, hunger/thirst 0..8, health 0..3 and herd milk 0..3. There are exactly two tools and no role carries both a tool and milk. Total generated milk equals herd-stored milk plus agent-carried milk plus delivered milk; owner attribution is preserved. Board stock never increases and bucket water enters only from the declared well. These are source-reasoned invariants; arbitrary trajectories and numerical parity have not run.

Bounds [-5,256] for observations and [-3, 6] for rewards are declared, not measured. Static/data checks do not establish numerical parity or successful play.
