# Escrow with forfeitable guarantees
Parentless proposal; related to generic bilateral escrow, reputation and assurance mechanisms. No claim of novelty or nonisomorphism is made.
Actions 0 replenish inventory, 1 post inventory to escrow, 2 pledge a bond, 3 withdraw one's entire offer and forfeit one's bond, 4 consume available inventory. Every fourth transition matches equal amounts of offers; unmatched offers remain locked. Each settlement refunds remaining bonds. Withdrawals pay a reward to the counterpart through bond forfeiture and lower persistent reputation; reputation changes the partner's later settlement value.
Private stock is not shown to the other role. Escrow, bonds, reputation and withdrawal events are public. Both agents determine the amount matched, while withdrawal can reduce the counterpart's pending delivery and supply immediate compensation. Stock, offers and bonds create opportunity costs and delayed effects. Consumption reward competes with commitments. Offers or pledges beyond capacity are discarded by clipping, an explicit storage loss rather than guaranteed conservation.
Reward equals 0.65 * delivery * credit + 0.3 * partner forfeiture + 0.4 * consumption - 0.35 * own forfeiture. Delivery, credit and forfeiture are at most 1; consumption at most 0.2. Partner forfeiture requires partner withdrawal, zeroing partner escrow and therefore delivery. Positive reward is at most 0.73 without partner withdrawal, or 0.38 with it; the lower bound is -0.35. No validation or learning claim.
