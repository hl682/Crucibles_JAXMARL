# Delayed irrigation commons
Parentless proposal; related to generic common-pool allocation and renewable-resource dilemmas. No claim of novelty or nonisomorphism is made.
Actions 0 idle, 1 open a persistent gate, 2 close it and conserve water, 3 pump extra, 4 harvest. Shared finite reservoir supply proportionally rations competing requests. Allocated water enters a one-step pipe; it affects soil and crop growth only on the following transition. Moisture has an intermediate optimum, so maximum extraction can suppress one's own crop while depriving the partner. Harvest resets accumulated crop.
The observation exposes shared allocation and both crop/pipe quantities but keeps the partner's soil private. Both agents change the shared reservoir and the other's later allocation. Reward is harvested crop in [0,1], minus at most one action fee (0.04 or 0.02), hence [-0.04,0.98] for reachable actions. Gates, soil, crop and pipe persist. This is a proposal only; no validation or learning claim.
